# Child Safety & Privacy Safeguards

EduKadence is engineered with zero-compromise child privacy and safety safeguards for minors aged 2–10 (complying with COPPA and GDPR-K principles).

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Child Safeguards

1. **No Third-Party Advertising**: Kid Mode contains zero external advertising SDKs, zero affiliate banners, and zero behavioral tracking.
2. **No Public Profiles or Leaderboards**: Children are never ranked against other children. Rewards are purely personal achievements.
3. **No Unsupervised Child-to-Child Communication**: There is no public chat, direct messaging between children, or public comment feeds.
4. **Restricted Media URLs**: Unlisted or private school media are never guessable or exposed to unauthorized users.
5. **No Exposure of Adult Data**: Kid Mode strictly blocks access to fee invoices, parent phone numbers, staff notes, and administrative settings.
6. **Parental Supervised Entry & Exit**: Kid Mode is initiated by authorized adults, and exit requires passing an adult verification challenge.
